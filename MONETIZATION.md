# Ghana Invoice Generator — Monetization Strategy

## Primary revenue

Free invoice generation supported by advertising (e.g. Google AdSense). Do not assume an account exists or is approved.

## Ad locations

Suitable: homepage, informational pages, template pages, guides, other public content pages.

Never: inside exported invoices; over the editor or preview; beside download buttons in a misleading way; where ads could be mistaken for invoice controls; where they obstruct printing or PDF export.

## Free features (always)

Create and edit invoices, add line items, calculate totals, preview, download PDFs, print, basic templates. No artificial restrictions.

## Future premium features

Premium templates, multiple saved invoices, invoice history, recurring invoices, branding, client management, payment tracking, reports. No complex payment system for the MVP.

## Affiliate revenue

Business tools, accounting software, hosting, courses, professional services — always disclosed.

## Analytics

Visitors, invoice starts and completions, template selection, PDF downloads. Never invoice contents or personal business information.

## Principle

The product must stay useful and trustworthy for free users. Never compromise invoice quality or privacy for ad revenue.
