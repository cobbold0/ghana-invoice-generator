# Ghana Invoice Generator — SEO Strategy

## Objective

Organic traffic from people in Ghana searching for free invoice tools and invoicing guidance.

## Search intent

Free invoice generator; online invoice generator; invoice generator Ghana; free invoice template Ghana; create invoice online; download invoice PDF; business invoice template; freelancer invoice template; how to write an invoice in Ghana; invoice vs receipt.

## Core pages

`/`, `/invoice-generator`, `/free-invoice-generator`, `/invoice-templates`, `/invoice-template-ghana`, `/how-to-write-an-invoice`, `/invoice-vs-receipt` — only with useful, distinct content.

## Content

How to write an invoice; what it should contain; invoice vs receipt; numbering; payment terms; invoicing for freelancers and small businesses; downloading and sending a PDF invoice. Do not invent legal or tax requirements.

## Metadata

Unique title, meta description, canonical URL, Open Graph, one H1, logical headings on every public page.

## Structured data

Where appropriate. Never fabricate ratings, reviews, testimonials, statistics or authors.

## Internal linking

Guides link to the generator, templates and related guides; the primary CTA is easy to find.

## Indexing

Index public informational pages. Never index drafts, user-specific previews, private data or temporary editor states.

## Principle

Every indexed page provides genuine value and leads naturally to invoice creation.
